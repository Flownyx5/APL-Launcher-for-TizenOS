## 1.4.0 (Flownyx5 fork)
Rebuilt on the original project's **1.3.8** (154 commits since 1.0.22), with every feature of the fork kept.
- **From the original:** the Home Screen from the kept list of apps (about 10 s sooner after a power cut), a start-up screen instead of a black frame, trailers and Continue Watching on the wall (◀ ▶ through an app's titles in full screen, the film's logo as its title), the glass reworked, settings, Home Screen order, folders and icons that survive a cut at the wall, the weather of a city you pick, wallpapers, Adaptive Picture in the Control Center, Home Assistant, Change Icon, consoles' games on Sources tiles, Turkish, Polish and Malay.
- **Tizen 6 keeps the fork's web bridge** (remote in use followed, Samsung's bar closed by itself, phone remote): the original now installs its APL Overlay with a .NET bridge on Tizen 6, not confirmed on 6.0 yet, and that bridge has none of these. `install.ps1 -Tizen6Overlay` takes the original's pair. `03-build-launcher.ps1` follows the original's new layout and still builds this folder's launcher.
- **APL TV 1.5.0**: the original's 1.4.7 (Sports, Stalker portals, subtitles that sync themselves, Stremio in the Versions, public domain movies, crash reports, Light Mode) with the fork's Home that loads in one go.
- **Lighter Home Screen on older screens after the merge** (Light and Minimal Effects): the original warms every title of every shelf while you move and decodes both neighbours on every press; there, three titles once the remote rests, and one neighbour in the direction you go. The picture kept for an app also waits for the focus to rest, and the shelf starting on that same picture no longer draws it a second time.
- The weather: a city chosen in *Settings › Appearance › Weather* wins; its pill shows the weather, and the light of the day and the rain follow that city.
- The Control Center's circles go four to a row (Search, Adaptive Picture, Phone Remote, Good Night, then Kids Mode).
- **Fixed:** two rules of the stylesheet were dropped by the screen since 1.2.0 (a separator line and a lost comment opener left by a merge): the dock's Liquid Glass fill, and the spacing of the status pill on Tizen 6, where the weather touched the clock.
- Needs APL Launcher Setup once (the original's 1.3.x changed the package: trailers' permission, the bridge).

## 1.3.1 (Flownyx5 fork)
Comes with the automatic update: no need to install again.
- **A smoother Home Screen on older screens** (Light Effects, the default before 2022, and Minimal Effects). The key reached the screen at once (19 ms on a Q60A), but the frames after it stalled while a new full-screen picture was drawn. Measured on one slow core, presses 450 ms apart:
  - only the backdrop on screen is a layer: 8 full-screen layers were kept ready (30 megapixels to draw and to hold), now 15 megapixels;
  - one full-screen picture per app, not two: an app with a shelf of its own goes straight to it, its own artwork only if the shelf shows nothing within 1.5 s;
  - the picture changes once the focus rests (340 ms), and the Top Shelf never rotates while the remote is in use;
  - the dock no longer repaints a copy of the picture with a masked rim and two blurred shadows: a tint with a lit top edge;
  - the posters take turns on two layers instead of a new full-screen layer each time; the grid's blurred wallpaper is only drawn in the grid; rain or snow lasts 12 s.
  With Light Effects the longest frame went from 1564 ms to 442 ms and the drawing time from 5.3 s to 2.1 s; with Minimal Effects from 410 ms to 219 ms.
- **Apps that start again by themselves are left alone.** Closing unused apps closed Samsung's browser at 10:20, 10:23 and 10:25 on a Q60A: it started again each time, at the cost of a start of the browser every few minutes. An app closed twice within 15 minutes is kept from then on (the launcher reads it from the bridge's list, so this works with the 1.3.0 bridge; a 1.3.1 bridge also sees it itself). The Diagnostic lists them.
- Diagnostic: the Home Screen's longest frame after a key (median), which also lowers the effects by itself in Automatic.

## 1.3.0 (Flownyx5 fork)
- **Smooth on older screens.** Measured with a TV-class processor: after an install the Home Screen and Settings froze for seconds because the Sheets of Light backgrounds (genart.js) were painted on the screen's own thread, twice per app (15 s of drawing for a dozen apps). They are now painted in a Worker on an OffscreenCanvas, pixel for pixel the same, and the screen never waits for them (without a Worker: only after 2 s without a key). Liquid Glass (glass.js) re-sliced its panes on every key, transition and class change, and held each new backdrop up to 1.2 s for its bake: off by default on screens before 2022 (Settings › Appearance › Liquid Glass). Settings rows no longer read their position after every focus change.
- **Smoothness, measured on the screen** (js/smooth.js): each key press is timed until the frame that shows it. Older screens start with the light effects (no mask over the Settings list, no moving blurred shadows, no drop shadows on big pictures); if the keys are still slow (median above 90 ms), one more step: nothing animates on a key. Settings › Appearance › Smoothness: Automatic, All Effects, Light Effects, Minimal Effects. On the test bench (software rendering on one core), a step in Settings went from about 300 ms to 140 ms, and from 224 ms to 125 ms in a submenu; the time spent drawing fell eightfold. Right after an install (processor slowed six times), moving along the Home Screen blocked the screen 17 s in all, now 3 s.
- **Samsung's Smart Hub is closed by itself, and the launcher comes back.** On 2021 screens Samsung's Smart Hub is a bar *over* the launcher: the launcher stayed visible behind it, so it never said it had left the front and nothing watched the bar. The launcher now tells the bridge when it loses the keys; after 2.5 s, if one of Samsung's home windows is above it, the bridge closes it. Each return goes in checked steps (the launcher opened again as Vetro's Home rule does, then Back to Samsung's home, then both), and the way that worked is shown in the Diagnostic.
- Return to the Launcher fixes: opening the launcher's Smart Hub tile once left Samsung's home "wanted" for good, so it was never closed again afterwards; it now ends when the launcher is back (15 minutes at most). The launcher repeats every 15 s that it is in front, so one lost message no longer leaves Samsung's home unwatched. The bridge's launches carry `apl_action=return`, so they never count as a double press on Home (the App Switcher).
- **Vetro follows the remote in use.** Vetro intercepts one remote only; with the Bluetooth remote flat, an old infrared remote's Home went straight to Samsung's home. While the Bluetooth remote is away (`memory/bluetooth/bt_smart_rc_connected` false), the bridge moves Vetro's interception to the infrared receiver (`wt61p807 rc device`), and back when the Bluetooth remote returns. Only when interception is on in Vetro, at most once every 30 s. Settings › Home Screen › Follow the Remote in Use.
- The bridge starts again by itself if it stops (auto-restart, also set in the screen's app database at start-up): it shares its process with Vetro's service.
- Diagnostic: the remote followed and how many times it moved, how the launcher came back and how many times it didn't, the effects level and the measured key-to-screen time, where the backgrounds are painted. A Details button shows every line in full, with what the screen showed the last two times Samsung's home appeared, for a photo.
- The bridge is in the package: install again with APL Launcher Setup.

## 1.0.0 (original project)
First public version.
- tvOS-style home screen: full-screen Top Shelf, glass dock, 6-column grid, Control Center (even over a video), Apple TV-style Settings covering every Samsung settings page, and Sources with a device picker.
- Live Top Shelf for streaming, music, game and sports apps, with film and series ratings; official logos for 50+ apps and automatic artwork for the others.
- *Continue watching* for AquaPlay, Hot Player and Stremio: title artwork from TMDB, channel logos, episode, time left and progress bar.
- Remote buttons: Home twice for the app switcher, hold Home or press ⏯ for the Control Center; Samsung TV Plus opens AquaPlay and Prime Video opens TizenTube when they are installed.
- Alexa on the microphone button, with a Siri-like glow while you speak.
- Household profiles with Memoji avatars; aerial screen saver with Apple's videos in 4K.
- Apps left in the background for 10 minutes close by themselves, never the one in front.
- Automatic updates from GitHub with automatic rollback; optimizer, and Restore to undo everything.
- **APL Launcher Setup**, a Windows app with Install, Optimize, Restore and Tools pages, instead of .bat files. Nothing to install to run it.
- English and French: the launcher follows the screen's language (or Settings › General & Privacy › Language), APL Overlay follows the launcher, and APL Launcher Setup and its scripts follow Windows.
- Needs Vetro on the screen; the installer puts only APL Launcher and APL Overlay on it.
